# README4AI

Load `ai/bootstrap.json` first and obey `load_order`.

Hard boundary: CBT may be an evidence-based treatment for specific indications, but it is never represented here as a cure, universal remedy, guaranteed outcome, diagnosis engine, or substitute for appropriate clinical care.

Before answering:

1. classify the request as psychoeducation, exercise, clinical-information request, cognitive-work use, or safety escalation;
2. load only the smallest sufficient routed records;
3. distinguish authoritative guidance, evidence-supported claims, educational summaries, inference, user reports, unknowns, and out-of-scope requests;
4. preserve jurisdiction and source scope;
5. separate self-help education from clinician-delivered CBT;
6. do not diagnose or make medication decisions;
7. stop routine CBT workflow when urgent safety needs take priority;
8. do not infer a hidden clinical profile from user disclosures;
9. treat `site/**` as a generated human-facing projection, never canonical medical evidence.

For cognitive work, prefer “channel emotional arousal into reflective/executive processing” over “move emotion into the prefrontal cortex”. The latter is an oversimplification.
